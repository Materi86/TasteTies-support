App data and Apple services

TasteTies normally stores recipes, photos, meal plans, shopping lists, settings, import history, timers and local diagnostics on your device. TasteTies has no developer account, recipe server, advertising or third-party analytics in the app.

CloudKit sync is off by default. If enabled, recipes, images, shopping items and sync metadata may be stored in your private iCloud database. Sharing makes the selected content available to participants according to their permissions. Turning sync off stops new sync operations without automatically deleting cloud data. Optional iCloud Drive backups are separate. They contain database records and omit image files, meal plans stored outside the database and drafts.

Apple provides iCloud, notifications, WatchConnectivity and StoreKit. Sync can use silent push notifications, and selected cooking, timer or shopping data can go to a paired Apple Watch. Apple handles App Store purchases. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Import, export and sharing

When you import a URL, your device contacts that website directly. The site may receive your IP address, the requested URL and normal connection details. Image text recognition runs on your device. The app uses camera, photo, file and notification access when you use the relevant feature and grant the permissions required by iOS.

Exports and QR codes may contain recipe data, images or sharing links. Recipients and the services you select handle copies under their own terms.

Support and this website

If you email support, we use your address, message and any attachments to respond and follow up. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing support and resolving problems. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the issue, and longer only for legal obligations or claims. Microsoft Outlook handles support email. See Microsoft’s Privacy Statement.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for information including retention and international transfers. The site has no analytics, advertising or contact forms. Your theme preference is stored locally in your browser.

Purpose, retention and your rights

TasteTies processes data to provide the functions you choose. When processing personal data is necessary to provide the app or a feature you request, the legal basis is performance of the contract with you. You choose whether to enable cloud features.

Local data normally remains until you delete it, reset app data or uninstall the app. System backups, iCloud data, exported files and shared copies must be managed separately. CloudKit and iCloud Drive backups also need to be managed separately from each other.

Contact taste-ties@hotmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objecting to processing based on legitimate interests. Your rights depend on the processing involved. You may lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection.
